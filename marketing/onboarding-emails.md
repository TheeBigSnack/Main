# Onboarding emails for pilot salespeople

Three short emails, sent by the pilot lead. Day 0 the morning they start, day 2, day 7. Fill in the brackets.

---

## Day 0: install and set-up (10 minutes)

**Subject:** Lot Sync: install and set-up, about ten minutes

Hi [name],

Welcome to the Lot Sync pilot. Two things today:

**1. Install (2 minutes).** Unzip the folder I sent somewhere it will stay, like Documents. In Chrome go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and choose the `extension` folder. Pin Lot Sync from the puzzle-piece icon.

**2. Set-up (5 minutes).** Open [dealership used inventory URL], click the Lot Sync icon, then **Set up Lot Sync**. The side panel walks you through: reading the website, your store, your name and role, the store's address (already filled from the website), permission for automatic rescans (say yes so your To do list stays current), and the posting rules. Please read the rules once; they are short and they matter.

That's it. Tomorrow's email is about your first posts. If anything doesn't work, reply with what you saw on screen.

Lot Sync never clicks Publish for you and never asks for your Facebook login. As the posting rules say, a person clicking Publish is the safest way to do this, not a guarantee. Lot Sync is not affiliated with Meta.

[your name]

## Day 2: your first posts

**Subject:** Lot Sync: your first posts, and one thing to check on Facebook

Hi [name],

**Before you post:** open Facebook Marketplace → Your listings → Drafts and delete any old drafts. Facebook sometimes puts a saved draft back onto a new listing form a few seconds after it opens. Lot Sync notices and fixes the fields, but it's cleaner without.

**One car:** click the Lot Sync icon on the inventory page, **Ready to post**, then **Post** on a car. Read the description in the side panel (edit it if you like), click **Open the Marketplace form**, check every field on Facebook, especially condition and title, and click Publish yourself. Back in the panel, click **It's posted, record it**.

**Several cars:** tick a few on Ready to post and click **Post N cars**. The panel loads each one; you click Publish on each. Skip, Pause and Stop are always in the panel. The daily cap is [10]; it's a safety setting, not a target.

If the panel ever shows **Couldn't fill**, copy the report with the button and send it to me. That is exactly what the pilot is for.

[your name]

---

## Day 7: keeping listings right, and the weekly check-in

**Subject:** Lot Sync: your To do list, and Thursday's check-in

Hi [name],

By now Lot Sync has re-read the website a few times. Click the icon and look at **To do**:

- **Take down** means a car you listed sold or went sale-pending. Click **Open listing**, then Mark as sold (or Delete) on Facebook yourself. The panel notices and ticks it off.
- **Update price** means the website price changed. Click **Open & update price**, click Edit listing on Facebook, and the new price is in the box; click Update yourself.

Same-day take-downs are the point of the pilot, so please clear To do items the day they appear.

**Thursday [time], five minutes:** click the **Pilot** tab in Lot Sync and click **Download CSV**, then send me the file (or bring it). It has how long your posts took, anything the tool couldn't fill, and how long items sat on To do. No customer data is in it. We'll go over it with [manager] on [day].

Thanks for putting up with the rough edges. Every report you send fixes something.

[your name]
