# Onboarding emails for pilot salespeople

Three short emails, sent by the pilot lead. Day 0 the morning they start, day 2, day 7. Fill in the brackets.

---

## Day 0: install and set-up (10 minutes)

**Subject:** Lot Current: install and set-up, about ten minutes

Hi [name],

Welcome to the Lot Current pilot. Two things today:

**1. Install (2 minutes).** Unzip the file I sent into a new folder that will stay put, like Documents\Lot Current (the zip holds the files themselves). In Chrome go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and choose that folder (the one with manifest.json in it). If I sent you a Chrome Web Store link instead, install from there and skip the unzip. Pin Lot Current from the puzzle-piece icon.

**2. Set-up (5 minutes).** Open [dealership used inventory URL], click the Lot Current icon, then **Set up Lot Current**. The side panel walks you through: reading the website, your store, your name and role, the store's address (filled from the website when it shows one; type any part it leaves blank), the price to post and the price note that goes into every description (a suggested sentence is shown; only your store can say whether it is true), permission for automatic rescans (say yes so your To do list stays current), the posting rules and the Terms. Please read the rules once; they are short and they matter. The price note can be changed later under Settings.

That's it. Tomorrow's email is about your first posts. If anything doesn't work, reply with what you saw on screen.

Lot Current never clicks Publish for you and never asks for your Facebook login. As the posting rules say, a person clicking Publish is the safest way to do this, not a guarantee. Lot Current is not affiliated with Meta.

[your name]

## Day 2: your first posts

**Subject:** Lot Current: your first posts, and one thing to check on Facebook

Hi [name],

**Before you post:** open Facebook Marketplace → Your listings → Drafts and delete any old drafts. Facebook sometimes puts a saved draft back onto a new listing form a few seconds after it opens. Lot Current notices and fixes the fields, but it's cleaner without.

**One car:** click the Lot Current icon on the inventory page, **Ready to post**, then **Post** on a car. Read the description in the side panel (edit it if you like), click **Open the Marketplace form**, check every field on Facebook, especially condition and title, and click Publish yourself. Back in the panel, click **It's posted, record it**.

**Several cars:** tick a few on Ready to post and click **Post N cars**. The panel loads each one; you click Publish on each. Skip, Pause and Stop are always in the panel. The daily cap is [10]; it's a safety setting, not a target.

If the panel ever shows **Couldn't fill**, copy the report with the button and send it to me. That is exactly what the pilot is for.

[your name]

## Day 7: keeping listings right, and the weekly check-in

**Subject:** Lot Current: your To do list, and the weekly check-in

Hi [name],

By now Lot Current has re-read the website a few times. Click the icon and look at **To do**:

- **Take down** means a car you listed sold or went sale-pending. Click **Open listing**, then Mark as sold (or Delete) on Facebook yourself. The panel notices and ticks it off.
- **Update price** means the website price changed. Click **Open & update price**, click Edit listing on Facebook, and the new price is in the box; click Update yourself.

Same-day take-downs are the point of the pilot, so please clear To do items the day they appear.

**[Day] at [time], five minutes:** click the **Numbers** tab in Lot Current and click **Download CSV**, then send me the file (or bring it). It has how long your posts took, anything the tool couldn't fill, and how long items sat on To do. No customer data is in it. We'll go over it with [manager] on [day].

**Weekly fixes:** what the check-in turns up gets fixed and comes back to you as a new zip. Follow the Update steps in `README.md`: unzip it over the same folder and click the reload icon on chrome://extensions. Your settings and your listings stay put.

Thanks for putting up with the rough edges. Every report you send fixes something.

[your name]
