# Dealer Subscription Agreement

Between [Lot Current entity name] ("Lot Current") and [Dealership legal name], [address] ("Dealer"), for the rooftop(s) listed in Schedule A. Effective [date].

## 1. Service

Lot Current provides the Lot Current browser extension and related services (the "Service") as described in the Terms of Service, which are incorporated here. In summary, the Service reads the Dealer's website inventory, helps the Dealer's authorised staff prepare Facebook Marketplace listings that the staff member publishes personally, and reports inventory changes. The Service never publishes, edits or removes listings by itself.

## 2. Authorisation to read the Dealer's website inventory

The Dealer confirms that it owns or controls its website(s) listed in Schedule A and **authorises Lot Current and its authorised Users to read the inventory data those websites publish** (including through the website's own inventory search service) for the purpose of preparing and maintaining the Dealer's own Marketplace listings. The Dealer will tell Lot Current of any restriction its website provider imposes on such access and will obtain any consent its website provider requires. Lot Current will move to an official inventory feed when the Dealer or its provider makes one available. [Attorney: see questions-for-attorney.md item 3 on Cars Commerce / Dealer Inspire terms.]

## 3. Authorisation for staff to post

The Dealer authorises the Users it designates (Schedule B, or as managed in the Service) to post the Dealer's pre-owned vehicles on Marketplace from those Users' own Facebook accounts, identifying the Dealer in every listing. The Dealer is responsible for choosing Users, for their conduct, for removing access when they leave, and for its own policies on employees' use of personal accounts. Removing a User from the Dealer's account in the Service ends that person's syncing with the Dealer's records, their view of those records in the manager view and their use of the description writer through the Dealer's account; it does not stop the extension on that person's computer, which needs no account to read the Dealer's public website and prepare a listing, and keeps the dealership name and role that person saved in it. [Attorney: see item 7.]

## 4. Dealer responsibilities

The Dealer is responsible for: the accuracy of its website inventory and prices; compliance of every listing with federal law and the vehicle-advertising law of every state where the Dealer is located or advertises (for a Pennsylvania dealer, the Automotive Industry Trade Practices regulations), including price display, fee disclosure and dealer identification, and with Meta's Terms and Commerce Policies; ensuring only pre-owned vehicles are listed; the daily cap it chooses for its staff, which each User enters in the extension's Settings; and reviewing every description before it is published. Lot Current provides checks that help, and does not replace the Dealer's judgment.

## 5. Fees

Per rooftop per month as in Schedule A, billed monthly in advance via Stripe. A free pilot period, if any, is stated in a separate Pilot Agreement. Prices may change with 30 days' notice.

## 6. Term and termination

Month to month from the effective date. Either party may terminate on notice effective at the end of the current paid month. Lot Current may suspend or terminate immediately for breach of the Terms of Service or the Acceptable Use rules. On termination the Dealer's database records are deleted per the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Data

The Dealer owns its inventory data and posted-listing records; Lot Current processes them to provide the Service under the Privacy Policy. Each User's local browser data belongs to that User's browser and can be cleared by them.

## 8. No guarantees; liability

Section 5, 10 and 11 of the Terms of Service apply: no guarantee of leads, sales or Facebook account status; service as is; liability limited to fees paid in the prior 12 months.
