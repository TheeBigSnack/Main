Website inventory: day 1
How to try it
  1. In the popup (the Lot Sync icon in the toolbar), click Scan website. The popup reads the sample dealership's used inventory and sorts it: ready, not ready, needs a look, skipped as new.
  2. Open Ready to post and click Post on a car. The side panel re-checks the car on the website, writes the description and checks every number in it.
  3. Click Open the Marketplace form. A sample form opens in a new tab and fills itself in, photos included.
  4. Check the form, then click Publish on it yourself. Lot Sync never does. Back in the panel, click It's posted, record it.
  5. In the popup: My listings has the car; the Pilot tab has the timing. Post a second car the same way, or tick two on Ready to post and post them as a queue.
  6. Click Day 2 up here, then Rescan website in the popup. To do lists the sold car and the price drop on your listings.
  7. Open & update price: on the sample listing click Edit listing; the panel fills the new price; you click Update. Open listing for the sold car, then Mark as sold. The panel notices both.

Every file of the extension runs unchanged; only the chrome.* API is stood in (demo/chrome-shim.js). Storage is in memory and empties when you leave. The daily post cap is 10, as in the default settings.
