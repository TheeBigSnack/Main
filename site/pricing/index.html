Pricing

Lot Sync is priced per rooftop per month, with a free pilot first; the planned prices are confirmed with you before any paid subscription starts.

  • five salespeople included
  • $20 a month for each extra salesperson
  • A 30-day free pilot first
  • Founding dealers: the first five stores pay $99 a month for their first year

These are planned prices. Pricing is confirmed with you before any paid subscription starts.

A rooftop is one store. Each person with the salesperson role takes a seat; managers don't. A subscription is started from the manager view on Stripe's own pages, where the price shows before you pay, and Lot Sync never adds seats or changes what you pay on its own (what managers see).

The free pilot

30 days, free, for 2 or 3 of your salespeople, with your used car manager's sign-off. We record how long each post takes, which fields the tool couldn't fill, and how long sold cars stayed listed, and we go over the numbers with you every week. No customer data is collected. At the end, the numbers are yours, whether you continue or not.

The pilot runs on your own website and your salespeople's own Facebook accounts, with every post published by the person posting it (how it works). Whether that is allowed on Facebook is answered straight in the questions and answers.
