How it works

  1. Reads your website

    Click Scan on your used inventory page. Lot Current lists the pre-owned cars at your store that have photos and a price. New, demo and loaner cars never get through.

  2. Fills in the listing

    Click Post on a car. The Marketplace form opens with the year, make, model, mileage, price, VIN, colors, body style, fuel, transmission, location, a description written from the website's facts, and the photos.

  3. You check it and click Publish

    Lot Current shows you what it filled, as the form shows it, and anything it couldn't. Then you publish, every time; Lot Current never does.

  4. Keeps listings honest

    With automatic rescans allowed, Lot Current re-reads your website every 3 hours while Chrome is open. Sold car? Price change? It shows on your To do list with a button that opens the right listing.

What you get

  • Pre-owned only

    Lot Current compares three signs on your website: the inventory type and the condition words in the car's web address and title. Before a car can be posted, at least two must say pre-owned, or one must and the website must link a Carfax report, and none may say new. A demo or loaner flag always means no.

  • Photos included

    The car's photos from your website go into the listing with everything else.

  • The website price, always

    The listed price is your website's price, on the price basis your dealership chooses. Price changes only mirror the website.

  • A description from the facts

    Written from the website's own data, with every number checked against it, and your dealership named in every one. You can edit it before it goes in.

  • Sold cars and price changes flagged

    Each rescan puts what to take down and what to reprice on a To do list, with a button that opens the listing ready to fix.

  • Several cars at once

    Tick the cars and Lot Current walks through them one at a time. Each one still gets your check and your click on Publish.

  • A record of every post

    The car, the listing link, the price, the time and who posted it. Marking a car sold or repriced ticks it off the list.

  • VIN check

    Before posting, the VIN's format, model year and manufacturer are compared with what the website says, and any difference is flagged.

  • A daily cap you set

    Your dealership sets how many posts a day each salesperson may make. It is a safety setting, not a guarantee of anything from Facebook.

Once the manager view opens, managers will see the post and to-do numbers for the whole store: what managers see.

What Lot Current won't do

  • Click Publish, Update, Delete or Mark as sold. Ever. There is no code for it, and a test that fails if any appears.
  • Post a new, demo or loaner vehicle, or any car the pre-owned check can't confirm.
  • Invent a price or a price drop. The listed price is the website price.
  • Hide that the car is at a dealership. Every description names the store and your role.
  • Ask for your Facebook password, read your cookies or messages, use fake delays, proxies or extra accounts.

Straight talk

Meta's Terms prohibit accessing its products by automated means without permission. Having a person check and click Publish is the safest design available; it is not a guarantee, and no tool can honestly promise one.

Lot Current is not affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc. "Facebook" and "Marketplace" are used only as the names of the places you post.
