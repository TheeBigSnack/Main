Legal documents

The Lot Current Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and the posting rules for salespeople, each on its own page.

All three are drafts under attorney review. The Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy are not in effect yet: nothing on those two pages applies to anyone until this note is gone. The posting rules' wording may still change, but Lot Current already shows them at set-up and before a salesperson's first post, and asks every salesperson to follow them.

  • Terms of service: the agreement between Lot Current, the dealership that subscribes and the people it authorises to use the service.
  • Privacy policy: what is collected, why, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to reach us.
  • Posting rules: the short page every salesperson reads before posting: you publish every post, pre-owned cars only, the website price, facts only.

To ask for a dealership's records to be exported or deleted, or for your own data to be forgotten, see privacy requests on the support page.
