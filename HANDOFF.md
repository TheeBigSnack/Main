## 2. The product rules (the short form; `CLAUDE.md` is authoritative)

Non-negotiables, enforced in code and by `test/posting.test.js`:

1. A person publishes every post and every edit. Lot Current fills forms and opens pages; it never clicks Publish, Update, Delete or Mark as sold, and never posts or edits in the background. `extension/facebook/formMap.js` has no selector for those buttons. The guard tests hold the form map to a list of what is allowed (known keys, fields and patterns), with a tripwire for Facebook's action wordings in English and Spanish; they fail if the fill code submits, or clicks or presses keys outside its helpers, or if any file reaches a page except through the known injected functions; and the e2e mock form's decoy buttons and action log catch a stray click at run time. They are text checks: deliberate obfuscation could get past them.
2. No detection evasion: no random "human" delays, no fingerprint/user-agent tricks, no proxies, no multiple accounts. Never ask for, read or store Facebook passwords, cookies or tokens.
3. Pre-owned only: `extension/src/classify.js` decides; new, demo, loaner and "needs a look" cars can't enter a posting flow; the side panel re-fetches and re-checks the car at post time.
4. Honest prices: the listed price equals the website price (dealer's chosen basis); price changes only mirror the website.
5. The dealership stays identifiable: every description names the dealership and the salesperson's role. First person is fine; posing as a private seller is not.
6. Facts only: descriptions state only facts in the source data; every number is checked against the source (`rewriteTemplate.js` `runGuardrails`).
7. Per-salesperson daily post cap (default 10; the dealership chooses it and each salesperson enters it in Settings), presented as a safety setting, never a guarantee.
8. No Meta/Facebook affiliation claims; "Facebook" and "Marketplace" only as plain names.

What runs on its own, only with the person's permission: re-reading the dealership website every 3 hours while Chrome is open to keep the to-do count current and, while the salesperson is signed in to a Lot Current account, sending that rescan's results (the posted list, post records, to-do items and the newest scan's counts, marked as held back when the scan looked like a website hiccup) to the dealership's account. It never touches Facebook then. Product copy, README and legal drafts say exactly that; don't bring back wording that says everything stays in the browser.

## 5. How the extension works (every flow, with the exact identifiers)

### 5.1 Storage (chrome.storage.local unless noted), all keyed per dealer website origin
- `settings:<origin>`: see `withDefaults` in `src/settings.js`: `{ version: 2, myStores[], storesChosen, basis: 'website'|'beforeFees', salesperson: {name, title, closingLine}, dealer: {name, city, state, zip}, priceNote, dailyCap, rewrite: {enabled, endpoint, key}, defaults: {titleStatus, condition}, autoRescan, notify, rulesReadAt, legal: {version, acceptedAt}, readySort: 'newest'|'longest'|'price'|'name', newDays, todoOrder: 'listed'|'oldest', cropBranding }` (`legal` since 0.5.0: the edition of the Terms and Privacy Policy accepted, `src/legalLinks.js` `LEGAL.version`, and when; both `''` until then, and nothing is recorded while `legalHosted()` is false; `readySort` and `newDays` (round K, `src/readyList.js`): the Ready to post list's order, set from its menu, and how many days a car counts as new, 1 to 30, default 7, from Settings' **Mark cars as new for N days**; both belong to the website and are not in the synced profile; `todoOrder` (round 8, idea I-17, `src/todoOrder.js`): the To do tab's take-down and price items, as the scan lists them (default `'listed'`) or oldest first by each item's open pilot flag; set from the **Order** control on To do when two or more items are listed; per website, not synced. `cropBranding` (dealer-branding round, section 24; default true, `s.cropBranding !== false`): crop the website's dealer branding off the photos in the side panel (`src/photoBranding.js`), from Settings' **Photos** box, **Crop the website's dealer branding off the photos**; a Settings form drawn with no store boxes (before any scan) changes neither, and the flag travels in the synced profile). Blank dealer city/state/zip are filled from the website's address at read time, and only from it: never guessed from a store's name (an install that already saved a guessed city keeps it).
- `snapshot:<origin>`: `{ version: 1, site: {origin, host, name, title, address, adapter}, takenAt, complete, vehicles: { [vin]: snapshotEntry } }` where `snapshotEntry` has vin, name, stock, url, price, priceLabel, priceBeforeFees, status, statusLabel, availability, location, locationShort, photoCount, mileage, type, carfax, dateInStock (the website's own in-stock date as it gave it, or null), decision, reason, blockers[], and (round K) `firstSeenAt`: when Lot Sync first saw the car, carried over from the last saved snapshot, this scan's `takenAt` for a VIN that was not in it, null on a first scan and for entries saved before the field existed (`rescan.js firstSeenAt`; nothing is "new" by first sighting on a first scan). A car that left the list keeps the page it was last seen on in `missingPages` (below) and, when it had one, its sighting in `missingSeen` (`{ [vin]: firstSeenAt }`), so a car that comes straight back is not a first sighting: its `firstSeenAt` is what it was, or null, never this scan's time. `src/readyList.js` reads the two dates: the website's first, else the sighting, each with its source in the words shown. `unchecked` (review round, only when there are any): `{ [vin]: reason }` for missing cars the last check could not confirm, which the next scan checks after the others (`scanRunner.js` `confirmOrder`), so a few failing pages never use up the check of a sold car behind them.
- `diff:<origin>`: from `diffScans`: `{ firstScan, unreliable, warnings[], takeDown[], priceUpdates[], newArrivals[], nowReady[], needsALook[], counts: { previous, current } }`, plus `takenAt` and `requests` from the scan runner, and (review round) `withheld` or `accepted` (below). Items carry `vin, name, stock, url, yours` plus `why/text/lastPrice` (takeDown; `why` is `'gone'`, `'sale-pending'` or `'not-pre-owned'`; a needsALook item about the salesperson's own listing whose details need a look has `why: 'review'`), `from/to/change` (priceUpdates, compared on the basis each listing was posted at, `postedBasis`), `what` (nowReady, from `whatGotReady`), `decision/reason/price/dateInStock/firstSeenAt` (newArrivals; the dates travel with the diff, which is saved even when the snapshot is not, so To do's date line never denies what the scan read). `unreliable` is set when a previous scan of 10+ cars lost more than half at once (`MASS_DISAPPEARANCE_SHARE = 0.5`, from `MASS_DISAPPEARANCE_MIN_LOT`): the popup, wizard and worker then keep the previous snapshot, and the diff keeps this scan's read as `withheld: { since, scans, cars, saved, snapshot }` (`rescan.js` `withWithheld`): `scans` counts the scans in a row whose reads agree (`sameRead`: both complete and not empty, differing by at most two cars or a tenth of the larger read, whichever is more), `cars` is the read's count and `saved` the saved list's; a scan that is saved holds nothing back. Once `WITHHELD_AGREE_SCANS` (2) agree, `withheldOffer` gives To do its **Use the new list of N cars** banner, and the click (`acceptWithheld`) saves `withheld.snapshot` as the snapshot and leaves the diff with `unreliable: false`, the hiccup warning gone and `accepted: { at, cars, saved }`, shown until the next scan's diff replaces it. While signed in, a held-back scan goes up with its own read's counts, marked `withheld` (`accountFlow.js` `scanFromStored`; `scan_summaries.withheld`, migration 0016). A posted car the website marks sold or sale-pending (`why: 'sale-pending'`) or now calls new, demo or loaner (`why: 'not-pre-owned'`: the listing is to be deleted, not marked sold; it gets no pilot flag, so no to-do item goes up for it) goes under takeDown, and one whose details need a look under needsALook (`why: 'review'`) unless the posted entry's `lookDismissed` holds that same reason; each is raised again on every scan until it is handled; a car whose own check failed (`confirm.unchecked`, a `{ [vin]: reason }` object) gets a needsALook item with the reason.
- `posted:<origin>`: `{ [vin]: { name, price, basis, postedAt, listingUrl?, salesperson?, postedWith?, updatedAt?, listedBefore?, userId?, mine?, lookDismissed? } }`, the listings that are up. `basis` is the price basis the price was taken on (`rescan.js` `markPosted`, `postedBasis`; a change of Price to post is for new posts, so a listing keeps its basis). It syncs with the listing (`listings.basis`, migration 0015): the sync function writes it when it adds the row or into a row that has none, never over one, and `mergeRegistry` takes the row's basis, so every computer prices the listing on the same basis. An entry with none (posted by an older build, or brought from a row that has none) gets one from the first scan taken once the listing had its price where only one website price equals it (`withSeenBasis`, `scanCar`, kept by `scanRunner.js` `keepSeenBasis` after the popup's, the wizard's and the worker's scans), and the next sync sends it into the row; with no such scan the current setting stands. `lookDismissed: { reason, at }` is a Dismiss on the person's own listing's Needs a look item (`markLookDismissed`): the item stays off To do while the website gives the same reason, and it is never synced; `listedBefore: true` marks a listing Mark posted recorded as made by hand before that day (no post of that day: the cap, the sync function's day count and the manager's posted counts leave it out); an entry merged in from a colleague by sync carries their `userId` and `mine: false`. "Mark posted" (by hand) records no listingUrl; the panel's confirm-posted records the link only when it is a listing's own address (detected after the page was seen to show the car, or pasted).
- `boilerplate:<origin>`: lot-wide description sentences to strip.
- `postQueue:<origin>`: `{ vins[], index, status: 'running'|'paused'|'done', results: {vin: 'posted'|'draft'|'skipped'|'blocked'}, dealerTabId, windowId, startedAt }`.

A take-down or an unmark leaves it as it is, so neither hands a post back; Mark posted on a car already on today's log (`alreadyLive`) adds nothing; each write keeps only the day's entries. `dayLog` carries in the day's posts the log never had (from the posted list and the take-down record, listed-before ones excluded). Never synced; while signed in, the cap also takes the sync function's count of the day's posts (`postsToday` in `sync:<origin>`).

### 5.3 Scan (popup "Scan website", the wizard, and the worker)
`performScan({ tabId, origin, settings, settingsFromProfile, snapshot, posted, boilerplate })` in `src/scanRunner.js`: `probeTab(tabId)` injects the neutral `probeSiteInPage` (MAIN world: the site name and the schema.org address), then each adapter's `probeInPage` in `ADAPTERS` order until one answers with its service (`{ site, service, adapterId }`); `detectAdapter(probe)` (none: `UNSUPPORTED_MESSAGE`, which lists every adapter's name); `search = searchViaTab(tabId, adapter, service)` (each request is made inside the dealer tab by the adapter's `searchInPage`); `scanWithSearch({ adapter, search, site, settings, prevSnapshot, posted, options: adapter.scanOptions(service), boilerplate })` runs `adapter.scan(search, { ...options, confirmVins, confirmUrls, lastSeen, postedVins })`, normalizes, settles the store labels over the lot (`shortLocation`), assesses (`assessVehicle`), builds the snapshot, `diffScans`, and the boilerplate; on a first scan or profile-seeded settings it settles the store choice (`defaultStores`; an empty `myStores` saved with `storesChosen`, a choice of every store, is kept) and re-assesses; then `rememberSite(origin, {...})` records the adapter, the service and `photoOrigins` for background rescans. The popup, the wizard and the worker keep the previous snapshot when `diff.unreliable`. The worker's `runRescan` calls the same `scanWithSearch` with `adapter.makeDirectSearch(service)`.

What carries from one scan to the next (`scanWithSearch`, `snapshotOf`): each car's `firstSeenAt` (from the last saved snapshot, `rescan.js firstSeenAt`; a scan whose snapshot is not saved changes no dates, since the next scan carries from the saved one again), `confirmVins` (the last snapshot's VINs and the posted ones), `confirmUrls` (each car's `url` from the last snapshot, plus its `missingPages`: the last page of every such car that was not in that scan, so a posted car that left the list on a bad server day can still be checked at its page) with each such car's `firstSeenAt` in `missingSeen`, `lastSeen` (the last snapshot's entries) and `postedVins`. A scan result's optional `unread` (cars the website still lists whose details could not be read this time) keeps those cars' entries from the last snapshot unchanged. The lot-wide description lines (`boilerplate:<origin>`): only a scan that is complete, not unreliable, skipped no description (`res.descriptionsUnread`, `res.unread`) and read at least `MIN_BOILERPLATE_COUNT` (3) works them out from scratch; any other scan keeps the saved lines and adds a line only when it is on 3 cars and 30% of the whole lot (the cars it did not read count in the lot, and an unreliable scan is measured against the last saved lot size); a car with no description that is not listed as unread counts in neither. An incomplete scan puts `incompleteWarning(res)` first on the to-do list.

`adapters/dealerInspire.js` `scan(search, { status, vins, confirmVins, fullRecords })`: POSTs `{ type: ['Used','Certified Used'], perPage: 200, page, sort..., filters }` (fields listed in `FIELDS`), walks pages, retries in other sort orders when paging is unstable, de-duplicates by VIN, then looks up VINs from the previous snapshot that the paged search missed (`confirmVins`, batches of 50) so a car is only called "gone" when a direct lookup fails too (`confirm: { checked, notFound, error }`). `getDetails(search, vin)` fetches one full record (all photos, description, features) for post time. `makeDirectSearch(service)` is the same POST from the worker (verified live: the Cars Commerce service answers direct requests from outside the page with HTTP 200). Its probe reads `window.SEARCH_SERVICE` (`search` URL, `apiKey`, `visibleStatusValues`) and searches through the page's own `IDPSearchServiceHelper.getListings` or a fetch with `x-api-key`.

`adapters/schemaOrg.js` (last in `ADAPTERS`) reads any website that publishes schema.org vehicle markup, through the shared readers `schemaOrgParse.js` (`parseVehiclePage(html, pageUrl)` → `{ vehicles, facts }`; pure, string-level) and `schemaOrgNormalize.js` (`normalizeVehicle(node, { url, facts })`; a price only when it is one US-dollar offer the page's visible text also shows as its current price, never crossed out or after Was/MSRP; no `priceCurrency` counts as dollars only when the page shows the amount with a dollar sign). `probeInPage` answers on a list of cars or one car's page, never on Facebook (`{ kind: 'schemaOrg', origin, listUrl }`); `search({ url })` is one same-origin GET (`searchInPage` in the tab, `makeDirectSearch` without cookies from the worker). `scan` walks `listUrl` and its rel=next pages, the robots.txt sitemap filtered to this lot's own car-address shape when the list shows fewer cars than its count, then each car's page two at a time (`CONCURRENCY`, no pauses); a 403, 429, 503 or bot check stops it, and so do three car pages in a row that fail (`MAX_FAILED_IN_A_ROW`). At most `MAX_CAR_PAGES` car pages a scan: posted cars first, then the oldest `pageReadAt`. Later scans read only new, posted, unpriced or changed cars (`lastSeen`, `postedVins`); a car whose page fails keeps its last reading (`unread`), or on a first read takes the list's data. A missing car is checked at its last page (`confirmUrls`): gone only on 404/410 or SoldOut, or (once the site's car pages are known to carry their own VIN) a redirect away or the site's own data without it; anything else sets `confirm.error` and nothing is marked gone. `getDetails(search, vin, { url })` reads the car's page first. Tested only on synthetic pages (`test/fixtures/structured/`, `test/helpers.js standardSite`, `test/e2e/mock-standard-site.mjs`, the sandbox's second sample); no platform is claimed.

### 5.4 The pre-owned gate (`src/classify.js`)
Three signs from the website are compared: inventory type ("Used"/"Certified Used"/"New", or a schema.org itemCondition value such as https://schema.org/UsedCondition), the condition word in the vehicle page URL, and the condition word at the start of the title. Demo/loaner flags always win (sold as new). A demo or loaner named after the model year in the title, trim or page address, or after a " - " or " | " in the title, goes to `review`, and so does a standard-data car whose own name says demo or loaner while the page title says Used. "Courtesy" counts there only as a courtesy car, vehicle or loaner, and after a separator only at the end of the title or before "for sale" and a place, so a dealership name such as "Courtesy Motors" or "Courtesy Car Center" never counts. On standard-data websites a demo, loaner or courtesy word that only the page title has, before or after the model year, reaches the gate even when the car's name says Used (`schemaOrgNormalize.js`), so a page title that opens with a dealership name containing "Courtesy" sends the car to review. A car the website lists as damaged or refurbished (the words, or schema.org's DamagedCondition/RefurbishedCondition) goes to review ("The website lists it as damaged (inventory type). Check its condition before posting."); a new car stays new. One sign alone, a schema.org value included, still needs a second sign or a Carfax link. A Carfax link supports pre-owned but its absence never blocks. Mileage never makes a car "used" (the live site has "New" units with thousands of miles). Disagreement or oddity -> `review` ("Needs a look"). A trailer, RV, powersport vehicle or boat (`notACar`, from `listingData.js`'s `readVehicleKind`: the body style's words, where a car body style such as "Crew Cab Pickup - Trailer Tow" always wins, except a Van or Cargo body whose make is an RV or trailer maker's (a camper van or cargo trailer goes to `review`); only with no body words, a make that builds only that kind, matched on `makeKey`, which drops punctuation and company words so "Thor Motor Coach, Inc." is "thor" and "Can-Am" is "can am", as the whole make or its first words) goes to `review` with the reason in the website's words, and `recheck` says the same at post time; a new one is still skipped. `FORM_KINDS` (car/truck, motorcycle) are the only vehicle types the form is filled in for. Ready check: photos, a price, on the lot, not sale-pending, at one of `myStores` (none ticked: every store counts). Blocker codes: `no-photos`, `no-price`, `sale-pending`, `not-on-lot`, `other-store`.

### 5.5 Post flow (side panel, `sidepanel.js`)
Popup "Post" -> `chrome.sidePanel.open` (must be straight from the click) -> writes `postRequest` -> panel `startFlow` (or the panel's own list, round L, section 15.13: `postFromList` with `dealerTabId: null`): `readCarForPost({ tabId, origin, info: sites[origin], vin, url })`, which is `fetchVehicleDetails(dealerTabId, vin, { url, origin })` through the tab when Lot Current can use it (a tab that answered is final; a closed tab, one showing another website or Facebook, an address Chrome doesn't show, a page no adapter reads, or a probe that sees another origin answers `tabUnusable` and falls through; a same-site page that shows another list, or a list read only in part, is read again through that tab with the last scan's list), else `fetchVehicleDetailsDirect` (the adapter's `makeDirectSearch`, only with `siteReadOrigins` granted; a word that maps to nothing stays blank and the fields table shows the website's words from `source`, VIN check, "Check VIN online" via NHTSA with an optional-permission request), "Open the Marketplace form" (shut while `fillBlocker` finds a posting-rule problem, `ruleProblems` over `RULE_PROBLEM_CODES`, which holds for every fill path; first `carStillCurrent` reads the car on the website again when `readIsOld`: the read is older than 10 minutes, `READ_MAX_AGE_MS`, or a newer scan no longer lists the car or lists it at another price, status, availability or type) -> opens `FORM_MAP.createUrl` -> `runFill` injects `fillFormInPage(map, data)` then `attachPhotosInPage` with photos downloaded by the worker (the Facebook page can't fetch the dealer's image host; ### 5.6 Queue

### 5.7 First-run wizard (`wizard.js` and `src/wizardSteps.js`, steps `welcome, scan, store, you, account, address, price, permission, rules, terms, done`; `account` only when `accountsConfigured()`)
Offered by the popup's To do tab (`#setup`, `data-action="setup"`) until `wizardDone:<origin>` exists or the person clicks "Not now" (`skipSetup`); shows "Continue set-up" when `wizard:<origin>` is active (that hands the wizard a live dealer tab id, which matters after a Chrome restart). Starts from the website's saved settings, else the synced profile, else defaults (running or quitting it resets nothing), reads the website, pre-ticks the store from the site name, takes name/role, then (only when `accountsConfigured()`; `wizardSteps(false)` is the list without it; `src/accountConfig.js` names the production project, so the shipped build has eleven steps) the Account step: `accountStepModel` from `src/wizardSteps.js` in Settings' words; signed out, the email box, **Send me a sign-in code**, the code box and **Sign in** (`signInStart` / `signInFinish` with `{ config: ACCOUNT, storage: chrome.storage.local }`); a sign-in points `wiz.settings.rewrite.endpoint` at `rewriteEndpointFor(ACCOUNT)` (`rewriteAtAccount`; `finish()` saves it); signed in, "Signed in as <email>", the invite code box and **Join** (`redeemInvite(code, the You step's name, { url, anonKey, session })`), then "Joined <dealership> as <role>.", or the server's sentence ("that invite code is not valid") with the box left for another try; Next reads "Skip for now" until signed in and always goes on; the saved state keeps `account: { email, note, joined }`, never the session. ### 5.8 Automatic rescans (`background.js`, `rescanSchedule.js`)
`chrome.alarms` `lot-sync-rescan`, period 180 minutes, created on install/startup and by `ensureAlarm`. On alarm: for each `sites[origin]` with `auto`, if `isDue(latestOf(lastAttempt, lastScan))` (period minus 5 minutes slack), `runRescan(origin, { reason: 'alarm' })`: requires the entry's service, an adapter, `chrome.permissions.contains` for the origins, and existing `settings:<origin>` (never invents defaults, never writes settings); `scanWithSearch` with `adapter.makeDirectSearch(service)`; saves diff and boilerplate (snapshot only when not unreliable); `updateBadge` (sum of `todoCountFor(diff)` over all sites: the person's own take-downs, price updates and needs-a-look items); a desktop notification (`chrome.notifications.create`, icon `icons/icon128.png`) when the count rose above the person's outstanding list (`todoCountFor(previous diff)`), `settings.notify !== false`, and the reason is the alarm. Failures are recorded as `lastAttempt`/`lastError` and the popup shows them on To do (`#scheduleWarning`), including "no permission" with an "Allow automatic rescans" button. Settings also has that button; ticking the rescan box asks Chrome from the Save click and stays off if refused.

### 5.9 Listing upkeep (`upkeep.js`)
Popup To do: "Open listing" (take-down) or "Open & update price" (`data-action="upkeep"`, `data-kind`), which writes `upkeepRequest` (`kind: 'takeDown'|'price'`, price, listingUrl, name, listedPrice). For a price update the panel first reads the car on the website again (`upkeepPriceNow`, through `readCarForPost`, then `recheck`): it stops, opening nothing, when the read fails or is refused, or the car is gone, sold or sale-pending, no longer pre-owned, needs a look, has no price or is back at the listed price (another store, no photos or not yet on the lot do not stop it: an owner question), and otherwise fills the website's current price on the listing's own basis. The panel opens the saved listing link (a saved link that is not a listing's own address counts as none; with none, `FORM_MAP.yourListingsUrl` with a note) in a new tab and polls it every 1.5 s with `readListingInPage(map, LISTING_SIGNS, expectFor(map))`, which reads the page's static text (buttons, links, menus, tabs and dialogs skipped; the sold sign only in chunks of 40 characters or less) and returns, among others, `matchesId`, `matchesName`, `matchesPrice`, `matchesVin`, `hasPriceBox` and `priceBoxValue`. Nothing happens unless `onListing(seen, { id, yourListingsUrl, namesakes })` says it is this car's own listing: with the listing id known, only `matchesId`; never the Your listings page; otherwise `matchesName` and `matchesPrice` (every word of the car's name and its listed or new price), plus `matchesVin` when `namesakesOf` finds another car, posted by anyone at the dealership or taken down in the last 30 days, whose name contains this car's name or is contained in it. Otherwise the panel says which listing to open and what it looks for. Price: when this car's edit form shows a Price box, `fillPriceInPage(map, price)` sets it (up to 4 times if the loading form overwrites it) and reads it back; when the box is gone and the page shows the new price, the item is finished (`markPriceUpdated`, dropped from `diff.priceUpdates`). Take-down: finished when sold/unavailable flips from false on the first read of that page to true (`markTakenDown`, dropped from takeDown/priceUpdates/needsALook). "I updated it" / "I took it down" finish by hand; "Not now" cancels. The panel refuses an upkeep request while a post is under way (steps checking/review/filling/probe/publish) and stops a waiting upkeep when a post or the wizard starts. Each panel instance acts only on requests for its own window and ignores requests older than 10 minutes.

### 5.10 Descriptions
`description.js`: the site's `description` field mixes a lot-wide disclaimer, raw equipment dumps and sometimes a real write-up; `findBoilerplate` finds sentences on 30%+ of the lot and on at least 3 cars (`MIN_BOILERPLATE_COUNT`, so a 2- or 3-car lot keeps its write-ups); `cleanDescription` sends the write-up's segments as written, from the first, up to the first one it leaves out (lot-wide text, a Carfax or arrival label, an award line, a list of equipment), and drops the last one sent when it may run on into the one left out; bullets and headings go as written. Only the rewrite service gets that narrative. `rewriteTemplate.js` `buildTemplateDescription({ vehicle, dealer, salesperson, priceNote, highlights, stores })` takes no write-up: it writes 60–120 words (`WORD_LIMITS`; the barest car the gate allows still gets 65) in first person from the car's listed facts, with 4–6 features (the priority list, or the salesperson's highlights), the price note, the sign-off "I'm [name], [role] at [dealer]" (or the car's store), the closing line and a VIN line (`ensureVinLine`). `runGuardrails(text, { vehicle, dealer, salesperson, priceNote, price, closingLine })` checks every number against the source, the price and mileage however written, price-change wording, claims the website doesn't make, one owner without the Carfax flag, banned phrases (private-sale and steer-away wording included), the dealership, the role, the price note and the VIN line; the codes in `RULE_PROBLEM_CODES` block every fill (`fillBlocker`), while length, ALL CAPS and emoji only warn. The hosted function (`supabase/functions/_shared/guardrails.ts`, `rewritePrompt.ts`) and `backend/` run the same rules, and `npm test` keeps them equal. `rewriter.js` asks the rewrite service when Settings enables it (not with no dealership name, and not for a car at another store whose name lacks the town), and falls back to the template when the draft fails a check or the service can't help.

## 6. What the live Facebook form taught us (six live runs on 2026-09-27; all handled in `fillForm.js`)
1. Dropdowns are slow, custom comboboxes that open one at a time; the option list is drawn late. The fill waits up to 6 s per popup, scopes the option search to the open popup, and retries a dropdown on the spot when its read-back disagrees.
2. Price and mileage are reformatted by the form (commas); the read-back accepts digit-equal values.
3. Location suggestions include same-named towns in other states ("Waynesburg, Ohio"); the fill picks the suggestion matching the dealer's state (`locationExpect`), types the ZIP when known, and the state comes from the site's address. Sending Escape to the location box clears it: Escape is only ever sent to `document.body`.
4. **Facebook restores a saved draft ("2020 Honda Accord EX-L") a few seconds after the create page opens, over whatever was filled.** The fill waits until the identity fields have been still for 2 s (up to 6), fills the VIN first, re-checks every field after `recheckMs` (3 s) and re-applies anything changed underneath, and the panel names what Facebook changed and asks the person to delete that draft (Your listings -> Drafts). The owner's stale draft may still exist; ask them to delete it.
5. Make and Model are typeaheads that show default suggestions (Honda, then Accord) and **commit the first suggestion on blur**. They are typed one key at a time, only a suggestion containing the typed text is picked, the box is closed with Escape (never blurred). Make may be a dropdown-style control on some loads.
6. The clean-title control is a checkbox labelled by nearby text; the photo limit text reads "up to 20 photos"; the read-back of a box that empties itself after a pick uses the visible text beside it.
7. After these fixes, single cars and a queue ran with nothing under "Couldn't fill" (`formMap.js` `verifiedAgainstFacebook: 'live runs 2026-09-27, all fields'`).

Not yet verified on the live site: `yourListingsUrl` (`/marketplace/you/selling`), the listing page's sold/removed wording (`listingSigns.js`), the edit page's URL shape (the code accepts the listing id anywhere in the URL, or the car's name on the page) and its Price box; whether the listing page shows the description's VIN line or cuts it at See more; the wizard's permission prompt; a real alarm rescan and notification.

### 24.2 How the check works

A photo vendor's overlay is a transparent image laid over every photo (or only the cover) at the same pixel positions, so pixels that keep the same colour in the same place across many different photos of a car are overlay, while the picture changes from shot to shot. Each photo is drawn at 256 pixels on its long side (`CHECK_LONG_SIDE`; a photo under 200 pixels on its short side, `MIN_SIDE`, is not checked). Photos are grouped by size; duplicates count once, and a group needs at least four different photos (`MIN_PHOTOS`) and the overlay in at least 80% of them (`TUNING.share`). Parts of the overlay that touch an edge become bands (with text or a logo in them, or very solid and cleanly ended), a frame (three or four sides), or the strip a corner logo sits in; the crop cuts each such strip plus a margin of two check pixels, never more than 30% of a side for a band, and keeps at least 60% of the photo (`keepArea`), or the photo goes as the website shows it, its note saying what stays ("Frame left on: cropping it off would cut too much of the photo"; a solid logo inside that can't be stepped around is "Logo still inside"). The note names what came off each side: the band sides, a corner logo for a side cut only to step around one (`markSides`), and a strip cut only to keep the photo's shape. Things that also stay put are told apart and left alone: a fixed camera or booth that makes the photos too alike (`too-alike`), a mark whose brightness follows each photo's exposure (part of the scene), and, beside a see-through band, a strip that does not follow the changing photo next to it. A photo that does not itself carry the overlay found is never cropped. See-through watermarks inside the picture are not looked for at all (one can't be told from the backdrop of a spot where every car is photographed), and branding on the car moves with the car, so it is never overlay.

A banner the website puts only on each car's cover photo can't show up in one car's photos. For that, each check (setting on, cover read) stores a 128-pixel RGB copy of the cover (`lotSample`) in `coverSamples:<origin>`, keeping the last eight cars, and the next car's check compares its cover with the other cars' covers of the same size (`otherCovers`); the same four-photo floor applies, so it starts working once three other cars' covers of that size are stored. The crop sent is `cropPlan` of the stored entry, used only when `matchesCheck` says the bytes are the ones checked; the panel cuts that sub-rectangle out of the downloaded photo and encodes it again, so the file sent is new: the website file's metadata (EXIF, IPTC and XMP, so any copyright notice, creator or credit written into the file) does not carry over, the part kept is compressed again (a JPEG or WebP at quality 0.92), and `createImageBitmap`'s default colour conversion turns a wide-gamut photo into sRGB. Nothing is drawn on it, but its pixels are close to the website's, not byte for byte the same; a photo that is not cropped goes as the website's own bytes.

### 24.3 What it was measured on

The spike's harness generated synthetic galleries in Chromium (bottom bands, top bars with bands, rounded and thick frames, corner badges and tabs, bands with shadows, logo tabs or a photo counter, see-through bands, cover-only banners with lots of 3 to 8 cars, re-sized and re-compressed copies, 16:9 and 2048-pixel photos, galleries of 3 to 40 photos; and negatives: varied scenes, studios and booths with posters, fixed cameras, overcast days, the same spot every time, letterboxes, duplicates, tiny and large placeholders) and checked every result against the overlay it drew. Over 81 scenarios with five different seeds, 6,945 photos: 0 false crops (a photo cropped that carries no overlay), 0 false flags on a clean gallery, 0 lies (a crop called clean with an opaque overlay pixel left in it); 3,042 photos cropped. The 423 misses are the designed ones: see-through watermarks in the middle, faint or in a corner (not removed), a gallery of three photos (too few), a band on 14 of 20 or 6 of 8 photos (under 80%), and a few photos of a see-through band at a fixed camera or in a gallery of six. A cropped photo loses 3.8% of its picture beyond the overlay on average, 11.9% at worst. Re-measured after the review round's last changes (the see-through band's reach and edge, 24.4): the same totals. Decoding took about 10 ms per 1024x768 photo and 25 ms per 2048x1536 one, and the check itself about 40 to 170 ms per car, on this sandbox. **These are synthetic galleries:** no real dealer gallery has been checked.
